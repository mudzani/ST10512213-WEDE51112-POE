Give a Rescued Animal a Home

Paw Haven Animal Rescue is a Johannesburg-based non-profit dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned and stray dogs and cats. Every animal in our care receives a full health check, vaccinations, and steriliation before being made available for adoption.
